The studio

A working pottery, not a paint-a-mug bar.

You get a wheel to yourself for the whole session, a bag of stoneware, and someone standing next to you until the clay stops fighting back. Everything you make is trimmed, glazed and fired here.

First pieces come out of the kiln about three weeks after you make them. We'll message you when yours are ready to collect.
